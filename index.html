About LJS Landscaping

LJS Landscaping is run by Lee and his team right here in Abingdon. We're the lads your neighbours recommend — the ones who show up on time, do what we say we'll do, and leave your garden looking better than you imagined.

Whether it's laying a sandstone patio, putting up new fencing, or giving your whole garden a fresh start, we treat every job like it's our own. That's why every single one of our Google reviews is 5 stars.

What We Do

Everything your garden needs — done properly, priced fairly.

Garden Design & Landscaping

Got a garden that needs a fresh start? We'll design and build your perfect outdoor space — from the first sketch to the final plant.

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Regular mowing, crisp edges, seasonal feeding — we'll keep your lawn striped, green, and looking the business all year round.

Hedge Trimming & Pruning

Overgrown hedges? We'll get them shaped up and looking sharp. Regular trimming or a one-off tidy — we've got you covered.

Patio & Sandstone Paving

Indian sandstone, natural stone, or block paving — we lay patios that last and look stunning. Just ask our customers.

Fencing & Boundaries

From 6ft closeboard to decorative panels, we build solid fencing that looks great and stands the test of time.

Turfing & Seeding

New turf laid on properly prepared ground, or seeding for the patient gardener. Either way, you'll have a lawn to be proud of.
